## Modifications

As the license requires, this pack states how it differs from the originals:

- Each model's embedded textures were re-encoded from JPEG to PNG and, where
  larger, downscaled so their longest side is at most the size given in
  `manifest.json` (`max_texture`), so that every DamnationX build can decode
  them.
- The files were renamed to the ids in `manifest.json`.
- Geometry, materials, UVs and node hierarchy are unchanged.
