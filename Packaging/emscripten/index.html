Privacy

Diablo II Sounds

Replaces the game's sounds with the ones of your Diablo II or Diablo II: Resurrected. Your game files are read in place: nothing is uploaded or copied, only the imported sounds are saved in this browser. Then select the pack in Settings > Audio > Sound Pack.

Diablo II

Uses d2sfx.mpq and d2speech.mpq, plus d2exp.mpq, d2xtalk.mpq and patch_d2.mpq of Lord of Destruction if you have them. Creates the "Diablo II" pack.

Diablo II: Resurrected

The folder with D2R.exe, .build.info and Data. Only the sounds are read from its data (about a minute). Creates the "Classic" and "Advanced" (remastered) packs, about 60 MB.
